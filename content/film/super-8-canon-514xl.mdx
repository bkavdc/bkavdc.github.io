Next to the 4x5, the **Canon 514XL Super 8 camera** is almost careless in comparison — handheld, motorised, built for movement instead of stillness. I keep it loaded for the days that don't feel like they need a tripod: a walk, a meal, a bar before it opens. What comes back isn't a single decisive frame so much as a texture — grain, light leaks, the particular colour that only comes from a small gauge of film running through an old motor.
