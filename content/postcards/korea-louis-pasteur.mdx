Two things share this page: an illustrated Korean postcard printed with the title *무한의 세계 — Infinite Adventure*, and underneath it, a postcard that actually travelled — addressed to me in Danshui, New Taipei, and franked with a **470-won Korean stamp marking 200 years since Louis Pasteur's birth**, cancelled by a Korea air mail postmark. Two versions of "postcard" on one page: the kind designed to be sold in a shop, and the kind that only exists because someone put a stamp on it and sent it.
