Large format is the slowest camera format I shoot. Every frame means setting up a tripod, focusing on a ground glass under a dark cloth, checking the bellows, cocking a **Copal 0 shutter**, loading a single sheet of film in the dark. By the time the shutter fires, I've usually looked at the frame for longer than most people look at a finished photograph.

That slowness is the point. A 4x5 negative doesn't forgive a careless composition, so the camera forces a kind of attention I don't always bring to faster formats — closer to the way I look at a street before I start sketching it than the way I'd hold up a phone.
