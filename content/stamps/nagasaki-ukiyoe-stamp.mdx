Japan Post has a long habit of putting fine art on definitive stamps, and this **110-yen** issue is a good example — an ukiyo-e print, close to the style of Kitagawa Utamaro, reproduced at stamp scale and cancelled with a **Nagasaki Chuo (長崎中央)** postmark. Next to it on the same envelope sits a red commemorative cachet for Urakami Cathedral. A single corner of an envelope, and it's already carrying three centuries of the city's history.
