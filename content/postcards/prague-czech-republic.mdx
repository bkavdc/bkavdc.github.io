This one arrived through Postcrossing, card ID **CZ-2279775**, from a sender named Zdenda:

> "Hello Ray, my name is Zdenda and I live with my wife in Prague, the capital of the Czech Republic. My hobby is collecting comics. I also like genealogy — I've traced my family back several years. Take care, Zdenda."

It's franked with two stamps: a **10 Kč** stamp of a butterfly (*Colias myrmidone*) resting on flowers, and an **8 Kč** stamp of the plague column in Olomouc. Two small, specific pieces of the Czech Republic, chosen by someone I'll probably never meet, now sitting in a drawer in Taiwan. That's most of what I like about Postcrossing in one card — a stranger's actual handwriting, actual hobby, actual home city, arriving as a physical object instead of a message on a screen.
