The Taipei Water Museum's Baroque-Renaissance arches are the kind of building that rewards slow looking — repeating columns, a symmetry that's satisfying to lay out in pen before a single drop of colour goes down. I sat across the lawn for most of an afternoon on this one, ink first, a thin wash of colour after, letting the paper's white stand in for the midday light on the stone.
