Most of what I draw isn't finished scenery — it's a street mid-change, scaffolding up, a building half torn down and half still standing. There's something honest about drawing a place while it's under construction rather than waiting for it to be photogenic again. The board goes on one arm, the water jar balances wherever it can, and the sketch ends up looking a little like the site itself: unfinished, but clearly going somewhere.
