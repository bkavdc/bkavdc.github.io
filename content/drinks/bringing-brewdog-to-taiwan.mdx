After my time at Asahi, I moved to running **BrewDog's brand technical service and channel development in Taiwan, from 2021 to 2023**. It was a different job than keg maintenance — less about the gas line, more about the relationship between a brand and the bars, restaurants and distributors that carry it. Tastings, staff training, the slow work of getting a bottle or a tap handle onto the right counter.

## A sign, on the other side of the world

A while after that chapter ended, I was in Las Vegas — as a visitor this time, not on the clock — and walked past a BrewDog storefront lit up on the strip. I stood under the sign for longer than a tourist normally would. It's a strange thing, seeing a brand you once represented from behind a counter, now just as a stranger walking by. It's when I realized this was never only a job title on a badge. It was something closer to an actual interest in how beer moves from a brewery to a room full of people who want to drink it well.
