Czech Republic, **10 Kč**: the butterfly *Colias myrmidone* — the danube clouded yellow — perched on a spray of flowers, paired on the same envelope with an **8 Kč** stamp of the plague column in Olomouc. Nature and architecture, side by side in miniature, is a combination a lot of postal administrations reach for. It's part of why stamp collecting and postcard collecting have always felt like the same hobby to me, just at different scales.
