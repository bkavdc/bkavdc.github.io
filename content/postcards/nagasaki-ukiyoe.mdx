This card carries three marks worth reading closely: a **110-yen ukiyo-e stamp** from a series of Kitagawa Utamaro–style prints, a **Nagasaki Chuo (長崎中央)** post office circular date stamp, and a red commemorative cachet for **Urakami Cathedral** — noted on the card as having been called the best cathedral in the East before it was destroyed in the 1945 bombing and later rebuilt.

Underneath the postmarks, my own **"Happy Postcrossing — Ray K."** air mail sticker, the one I made for my own outgoing cards, addressed to New Taipei's Yonghe District. Japan's post offices are generous with this kind of thing — a commemorative cancellation mark for almost any landmark, if you know to ask for it at the counter.
