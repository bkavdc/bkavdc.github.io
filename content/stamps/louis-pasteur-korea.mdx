A **470-won stamp** from Korea, issued to mark **200 years since the birth of Louis Pasteur** — his portrait next to laboratory glassware, a small tribute to germ theory sitting in the corner of a Postcrossing envelope. Commemorative stamps are where postal history and general history overlap most directly: someone, somewhere, decided this was worth printing onto something people would lick and send.
