I'm in the middle of a move from Taiwan to the UK. It's the kind of change that makes you take stock: close to **8 years in the beverage industry** — draught systems, brand work, judging, studying — mostly built in Taipei bars, breweries and trade shows.

The UK is, among other things, a serious beer and cask-ale country, and I'm looking for the next role there that keeps me close to the same questions I've been asking for years: how a drink is made, how it's kept, how it's served, and how to talk about it clearly once it's in the glass.

Everything else on this site — the film, the sketches, the postcards, the stamps — has always traveled with the job, not around it. I expect that to keep being true on this side of the move too.
